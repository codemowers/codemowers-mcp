# Repository guidance

- Keep cluster capability descriptions and provisioning guidance composable. Describe only the resource or policy's own purpose, behavior and requirements. Do not assume other issuers, admission policies or optional platform components are installed or enabled, and do not redirect users to a named alternative unless its availability has been established. Discover available capabilities and applicable policy bindings before relying on them. Keep each resource's defaults and conventions in its own description instead of repeating them in unrelated descriptions.
